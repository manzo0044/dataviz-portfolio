THE GLOBAL ENERGY TRANSITION

Visualizing the shift from fossil fuels to renewable electricity (2024)

🔥 KEY FINDINGS:

  • Strong negative correlation (-0.810): Countries with high renewable electricity have dramatically lower fossil fuel use - the energy transition is measurable and real.
  • Nordic leadership: Norway (98% renewable) and Denmark (88% renewable) prove wealthy nations can nearly eliminate fossil electricity through policy and investment in wind/hydro.
  • Hydropower advantage: Brazil, despite being a developing nation, achieves 88% renewable electricity thanks to massive hydroelectric infrastructure.
  • Fossil dependence persists: Oil-producing nations like Saudi Arabia (98% fossil) and major economies like South Korea (60% fossil) remain heavily dependent on coal and gas.
  • The remaining 10%: Nuclear power fills the gap - countries don't need 100% renewable to eliminate fossils when nuclear provides clean baseload power.

Source: Our World in Data (2024), based on Ember Global Electricity Review & Energy Institute Statistical Review
